While I was at it, I did some serious tinkering under the hood to make searching for videos way faster. It's much smarter about how it grabs results now, so you should be finding your tunes in a flash!
Speaking of things that were broken, I also fixed a nasty crash that could happen when you tried to open the YouTube Downloader window. That's all sorted out now, so it should open without a hitch.
Here's a small but handy one: The main app window will now show the full, uncut title of whatever track you're playing. No more trying to guess the end of that super long song name!
And finally, I've swapped out the system that plays the app's little sound effects. The new one is much more stable, which means you shouldn't run into any weird crashes or errors just from the app making a sound.
new on 1.4.8:
You might have also noticed some of the keyboard shortcuts in the Audio Player tab weren't behaving themselves lately. That's all fixed now! Pressing 'T' to hear the time, using the arrow keys for volume and seeking, and Ctrl+P for play/pause are all working as they should again when you're in the player.
And for all you multilingual users out there, I've got a little something new. When you go to 'View Readme' or 'View Changelog' from the menu, the app will now try to find a version in your selected language! For example, if you're using Tunevia in Indonesian, it'll look for a readme_id.txt.
new on 1.4.7:
Ever wanted Tunevia to speak your language? Well, now it can! I've added multilingual support, so you can now switch the app's language right from the Options menu.
And for those who are tired of typing, give your fingers a rest because voice search is here! The app will ask for permission to use your microphone the first time, but after that, you can just speak your search. Yep, it's that easy.
I've also added a few little audio cues, like an intro sound when the program starts, with more on the way. And don't worry, if you prefer a quieter experience, you can easily turn all the sounds on or off in the new 'Sound Settings' section under the Options menu.
new on 1.4.6:
Ever wished you could quickly find something in your play history? Now you can! The 'History' tab now has its own search bar. Just start typing in the new search bar to filter your history by video title or uploader name. The list updates as you type, so you can find what you're looking for super fast. And don't worry, the program will let you know if your history is empty or if nothing matches your search. Plus, when you switch to the 'History' tab, the keyboard focus will land right in the search bar, ready for you to type.
And for Windows users, I've added a much-requested feature: global media key support! Now you can control playback (Play/Pause, Rewind, Fast Forward, Volume Up/Down) even when Tunevia isn't the focused window. These use the Alt+Shift modifier, so it's 'Alt+Shift+P' for Play/Pause, 'Alt+Shift+Left Arrow' to Rewind, 'Alt+Shift+Right Arrow' to Fast Forward, 'Alt+Shift+Up Arrow' to increase volume, and 'Alt+Shift+Down Arrow' to decrease it. These hotkeys are automatically set up when Tunevia starts and are removed when you close the app. the program will give you a heads-up (Through NVDA speech if you're using it) if there's any trouble registering them or if you're not on Windows. And if you press a global hotkey when no track is loaded or ready, the player will inform you with a message like 'Player not ready'.
new on 1.4.5:
Found the old playback speed steps too limiting? the dropdown in the Audio Player tab has been modifyed and it's now a slider! Now you get fine-grained control to set the playback rate exactly where you want it, anywhere between 0.25x (super slow) and 2.00x (double speed).
New on 1.4.4:
Added some handy keyboard shortcuts! You can now jump directly to the YouTube, Player, History, and Lyrics tabs using Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3, and Ctrl+4 respectively.
Improved keyboard navigation! Tabbing through controls within the main tabs (YouTube, Player, History, Lyrics), the Downloader panel, and the Lyrics Search Results window should now follow a much more logical path. Getting around with just the keyboard should feel more predictable and less like you're jumping randomly.
Also tweaked where the keyboard focus lands when you switch tabs or open the lyrics search results. It should now start you off on a more useful control, ready for action.
New on 1.4.3:
Added the ability to switch between different audio tracks if the video you're playing offers them (like different languages or commentary tracks). When multiple tracks are detected during loading, a new 'Change Audio Track' button will become active in the Audio Player tab. Clicking this button brings up a dialog showing the available tracks, including details like language, label, codec, and bitrate.
You can also set a preferred audio language now. Look for the 'Set Preferred Audio Language...' option in the main Options menu. When loading a new video, the player will try to select an audio track matching this language first. If a matching track isn't found, it will default to picking the audio track with the highest available bitrate, although this method also still not good for certain video's
New on 1.4.2:
Want to know where you are in a track without looking? Just press 'T' while you're in the Audio Player tab! It'll speak the current playback time and the total length (like "1:20 of 9:32"). If the time isn't available for some reason, it'll tell you that too.
Fixed that annoying thing with the keyboard shortcuts! You know how sometimes you'd try to adjust the Volume or Seek Interval sliders with the arrow keys, but it would rewind or change volume instead? Well, now when you have keyboard focus specifically on the Seek Interval slider, Volume slider, or the Playback Speed dropdown, the arrow keys will adjust that control, as they should. As soon as you tab or click away, the arrows go back to controlling player seek and volume.

New on 1.4.0:
Well, the program's got a new name! It's Tunevia now, 'cause let's face it, it does more than just YouTube stuff these days!
You can now have the next track from your search results play automatically in the Audio Player tab! Of course, there's a toggle in the options if you find that annoying.
Ever wanted to speed up or slow down your tracks? Now you can! Added playback speed controls (0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x) right in the Audio Player tab.
